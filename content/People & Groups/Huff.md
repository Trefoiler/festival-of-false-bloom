# Huff Huff Huff

**Updated through:** [Session 3](../Session%20Notes/Session%203.md)
**Index:** [People & Groups](index.md)

- **Identity:** Shopkeeper at Hanesworth Goods and Supplies. His name is written Huff Huff Huff; Nathan calls him Huff.
- **Shop:** Open despite a large hole from the festival blast.
    - Sold three empty vials for 2 gp each. Said he needed the money because of the damage and his wife's illness.
    - Sold Azier hand wraps for 2 gp after he negotiated. Had no shield or quiver to offer during the visit.
- **Connections:** Knows Tomas Reed through his visits to town and referred the party to [Toby](Tobias.md) by the river. Asked them to pass along his regards to Toby.
- **Desert:** Said he moved away when he was young, had not returned in some time, and remembered little about the strange happenings he mentioned there.
- **Last seen:** At his shop when the party left to rest.
- **Source:** [Session 3](../Session%20Notes/Session%203.md).
