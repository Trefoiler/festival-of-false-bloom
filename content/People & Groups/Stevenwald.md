# Stevenwald

**Updated through:** [Session 3](../Session%20Notes/Session%203.md)
**Index:** [People & Groups](index.md)

- **Identity:** Tomas Reed's ox; pulls the farm's supply cart.
    - Introduced as Stevenwald, one word with the ending spelled W-A-L-D during the session.
- **Recovery:** Two goblins were leading him away from the farm. They fled when Tomas charged out with his sword, and the ox recognized his owner.
- **River encounter:** Became unresponsive along with Tomas as shadows formed around them.
    - The ape searched the supplies he carried. Stevenwald recovered afterward without an established explanation for what happened.
- **Party connection:** Pulled the supply cart to Hanesworth with Tomas and the party. Gordon briefly perched on his back at the farm.
- **Last seen:** Leaving Hanesworth with Tomas to return to the farm after the hospital delivery.
