# Session 3: Back to Hanesworth & Dragon's Folly

**Date:** 2026-11-15
**Active players:** Owen, Shepherd, Brayden, Damane
**References:** [Party Members](../Party%20Members.md) · [Current Threads](../Current%20Threads.md) · [People & Groups](../People%20&%20Groups/index.md)
**Source:** Session notes and campaign handouts.

## Quick recap

- Tomas Reed lent the party the Stone of Change. The party agreed to keep it from Mayor Silas Pruitt and promised to return it to Tomas.
- The party delivered the medical supplies to Hanesworth's hospital and completed **A Hospitable Choice**, earning 2 Supplies and 12 gp.
- Silas gave EscaMundo a potion that changed his appearance for ten days without changing his stats. Their brief fight ended with both still alive.
- At Hanesworth Goods and Supplies, EscaMundo bought three vials for the unfinished Six-Shooter, and Azier acquired Reinforced Hand Wraps. The party took a long rest in town.
- On the way to the smith, two goblins cheated at Dragon's Folly and killed each other. The party took the real gold and split 28 gp four ways.

## Where we stopped

- **Gambling tent in the woods:** The party is partway to Tobias “Toby,” the smith by the river. Both gamblers are dead, the loot has been split, and no fight is in progress.
    - The party has not reached Toby. Continue along the river route to pursue **The Smith's Request** and ask about equipment and the remaining Six-Shooter parts.
    - Tomas and Stevenwald were heading back to the farm. The party still has the Stone of Change.

## Chronological summary

- **Taking custody of the Stone of Change:**
    - The party asked Tomas why he kept the stone in his sword. He said it kept the heirloom close at hand and did not make the sword stronger.
    - The party suggested the farm intruders might have wanted the stone and asked to hold it while investigating. Tomas reluctantly lent it on the condition that they return it.
    - The party returned to Hanesworth, where rubble was still being cleared and the damaged hospital was treating people. Tomas went toward the hospital and recommended the smith Tobias, whom he calls Toby.
- **At Mayor Silas Pruitt's office:**
    - The party agreed to keep the stone from Silas. Citizens were waiting outside, and nobody answered the party's knock.
    - Jorah slipped under an interior door as a rat and found Silas with a brown-haired woman. Jorah returned to elf form; the woman screamed, and Nathan opened the door thinking someone was in danger.
    - The woman hurried out after collecting belongings from the reception desk. Silas denied she was his receptionist; her role and relationship to him were not established.
    - When EscaMundo pressed Silas for the promised alcohol, he pulled out a drawer and broke a bottle that had been inside. Silas agreed to meet the party outside for the supply handoff.
- **The potion and supply handoff:**
    - Silas gave EscaMundo a dirty bottle that smelled like alcohol. EscaMundo took a sip and temporarily took on a woman's appearance; Jon set the duration at ten days and kept his stats the same.
    - EscaMundo confronted Silas without his axe. Silas cut him with scimitars and disengaged; EscaMundo pursued him and landed a punch. The fight ended without a death or arrest.
    - Hospital staff took the medical supplies from the cart. Silas paid the reward for **A Hospitable Choice**, and Gordon handed out 3 gp to each of the four characters.
    - EscaMundo took one bottle of the hospital's leftover extra-strength alcohol.
- **Shopping and resting in Hanesworth:**
    - At Hanesworth Goods and Supplies, Nathan asked shopkeeper Huff Huff Huff for six vials. Huff had three and charged 2 gp each, citing the damage to his shop and his wife's illness. EscaMundo paid all 6 gp and Nathan retained the vials for assembly.
    - Huff had no shield or quiver to sell and referred the party to Toby. The Six-Shooter was approved and entered in Party Inventory, but the entry did not mean it had been made.
    - Azier negotiated the hand wraps down from 4 gp to 2 gp. The wraps were added to his inventory.
    - The party completed a long rest in town, clearing everyone's temporary HP. The next morning Jorah saw the woman from the mayor's office return to the reception area wearing what looked like a wedding ring. He shared the observation with the party.
- **Tomas's departure:**
    - Tomas was getting ready to go home with Stevenwald and asked about getting his heirloom back. Nathan gave him an ordinary rock he named Greg and renewed the promise to return the real stone. Tomas agreed to leave it with the party.
    - The party set out for Toby. On the walk, Jorah identified poisonous Slytherberries that cause diarrhea and skin welts. Azier collected a handful.
- **Dragon's Folly at the roadside tent:**
    - Two goblins, Schmiel and Grimith, invited the party to play a blackjack-like game for a 5 gp stake. Nathan used Magical Tinkering to pass copper coins as gold and won the first hand. Schmiel won the next hand; Azier and EscaMundo lost their stakes.
    - Nathan noticed something off about the play. He let the disguise on his own coins drop while they were in the gamblers' hands, prompting an accusation between them. Hidden wooden cards fell out during the argument.
    - While the goblins fought, Nathan took one pile of coins and Azier took the other. Schmiel stabbed Grimith, and Grimith killed Schmiel with an arcane blast; neither survived.
    - The coin piles contained counterfeits. The party agreed on a 28 gp split of real coin and took the playing-card set and two bottles before stopping at the tent.

## Clues & open questions

- **The Stone of Change:** Jon confirmed in the opening recap that Tomas's heirloom is the stone the ape wanted.
    - Handout: [Stone of Change image](../Attachments/Handouts/Stone%20of%20Change.png).
    - Nathan's earlier examination suggested someone or something is trapped inside. The occupant, the stone's powers, and why it was sought are still unknown.
    - The party suspects the farmhouse attackers may have wanted it too. They have no proof, and the connection to the matching sigils is still unexplained.
- **The mayor's office:** Silas first called the broken bottle a gift from his wife and family, then said it had come from his parents.
    - Jorah saw what looked like a wedding ring on the woman. The ring does not establish whether she is married or what her relationship to Silas is.
- **Toby and the smith's tools:** Tomas and Huff both pointed the party to Toby by the river. The quest scroll describes the main workshop as destroyed; what Toby can supply or repair at present remains to be checked.
- **Huff's old home:** Huff said he left the desert when he was young and had not been back in some time. He mentioned odd happenings there but gave no specific lead tying them to the festival attack.

## Table notes & corrections

- **Six-Shooter ruling:** Once built and loaded, EscaMundo can use one bonus action and a free hand to draw, uncork, and drink one vial under his normal drunkenness rules. Refilling takes a minute or two outside combat. Three more vials and the bandolier hardware are still needed.
- **Gambling ruling:** Nathan used Magical Tinkering on five copper coins as a one-time exception. His usual limit remains four objects.
- **Sharing observations:** When a clue is given to one character, say aloud whether they pass it on. For the ring observation, treat it as shared.
- **End-of-session feedback:** Use passive checks more often when appropriate, let the player whose character initiates a check take the lead, and consider Inspiration for staying in character.
    - Players liked seeing the townspeople refer to one another and wanted more opportunities for everyone to participate and reveal pieces of their backgrounds during play.
    - The group liked having room for roleplay and suggested roughly one meaningful fight in a typical session. A separate combat practice session or one-shot was discussed as an option.
