### Typical turns

- **Ranged turn:** Lightning Launcher → reposition to maintain distance → command Gordon.
- **Melee escape turn:** Shocking Grasp → retreat up to 35 ft. if it hits → command Gordon.
    - A hit prevents that enemy's opportunity attack during Nathan's retreat. Other enemies can still react.
- **Healing through Gordon:** Cast Cure Wounds (Nathan's action + slot) → Gordon delivers it (his reaction) → command Gordon.
    - Gordon must already be within touch reach of the ally and within 120 ft. of Nathan when the spell is cast.
    - Delivery uses Gordon's reaction, leaving his action available for the commanded action.
- **Support turn:** Cast Faerie Fire or Grease → reposition → command Gordon.
- **Reliable escape turn:** Disengage → move up to 35 ft. → command Gordon.
    - Use when escaping safely matters more than attempting damage; no successful attack is needed.

**Gordon reminder:** Commanding him uses Nathan's bonus action, usually for Force Strike or Help. [Gordon](Gordon.md) takes his turn immediately after Nathan.

### Cantrips

> [!info]- Mending
> **Cast:** 1 minute · **Range:** Touch · **Components:** V, S, M
> **Duration:** Instantaneous · **Concentration:** none after casting; required during the minute of casting.
>
> - **Repair:** fix one break or tear in an object, no larger than 1 ft. in any dimension, leaving no trace of that damage.
> - **Magic items and constructs:** can repair physical damage, but cannot restore lost magic.
> - **Gordon:** restores 2d6 HP, up to his maximum. Does not recreate him after death.
> - **Timing:** not a one-action combat heal. The casting takes the full minute and interrupts an existing concentration spell.

> [!info]- Shocking Grasp
> **Cast:** Action · **Range:** Touch · **Components:** V, S, M
> **Duration:** Instantaneous damage; reaction prevention until the start of the target's next turn · **Concentration:** No
>
> - **Attack:** melee spell attack, +6 to hit. Advantage if the target wears metal armor.
> - **Hit:** 1d8 lightning damage; the target cannot take reactions until its next turn starts.
>   - Nathan or an ally can leave that target's reach without provoking an opportunity attack from it. Other enemies can still react.
> - **Miss:** no damage or reaction prevention. Disengage is the reliable escape option when a failed attack would be too risky.
> - **Through Gordon:** Nathan's action + Gordon's reaction. Gordon must already be able to touch the target and be within 120 ft. of Nathan. Nathan's bonus action remains available.
> - **Scaling:** 2d8 at character level 5, 3d8 at 11, 4d8 at 17. Do not add INT to damage.

### First-level spells

> [!info]- Absorb Elements
> **Cast:** Reaction when Nathan takes acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage · **Range:** Self · **Components:** S, M
> **Duration:** 1 round, with the timing below · **Concentration:** No · **Cost:** 1 slot
>
> - **Resistance:** halve damage of the triggering type, rounded down, including the triggering damage. Resistance lasts until Nathan's next turn starts.
> - **Stored damage:** the first melee attack Nathan hits with on his next turn deals an extra 1d6 of the absorbed type; the spell then ends. If no qualifying hit occurs that turn, the extra damage is lost.
>   - Shocking Grasp is a melee attack and can qualify. Gordon's own Force Strike does not.
> - **Reaction cost:** Nathan cannot also make an opportunity attack before his reaction refreshes at the start of his turn.
> - **Higher slots:** extra melee damage increases by 1d6 per slot level above first; the resistance is unchanged.

> [!info]- Cure Wounds
> **Cast:** Action · **Range:** Touch · **Components:** V, S, M
> **Duration:** Instantaneous · **Concentration:** No · **Cost:** 1 slot
>
> - **Healing:** a touched creature regains 1d8 + 4 HP, up to its maximum.
> - **At 0 HP:** healing wakes a living ally and resets death-save successes/failures. They remain prone. Cannot revive a dead creature.
> - **Excluded targets:** no effect on undead or constructs, including Gordon.
> - **Through Gordon:** he must already be able to touch the recipient and be within 120 ft. of Nathan. Nathan spends his action + slot; Gordon spends his reaction.
>   - No bonus-action command is needed for delivery. Nathan can still command Force Strike, Help, or another action that turn.
> - **Higher slots:** add 1d8 healing per slot level above first.

> [!info]- Detect Magic
> **Cast:** Action, or ritual with 10 additional minutes · **Range:** Self; senses magic within 30 ft. · **Components:** V, S, M
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes · **Cost:** 1 slot, or none as a ritual
>
> - **Sense:** detect the presence of nearby magic for the duration. The sensing area moves with Nathan.
> - **Inspect:** while sensing magic, use an action to see a faint aura around a visible creature or object bearing magic and learn its school, if any.
> - **Barriers:** blocked by 1 ft. of stone, 1 inch of common metal, a thin sheet of lead, or 3 ft. of wood or dirt.
> - **Ritual:** must remain prepared. Takes the additional casting time but saves a spell slot; concentration is also required during ritual casting.
> - **Limit:** does not fully identify an item's properties or instructions for using it.

> [!info]- Faerie Fire
> **Cast:** Action · **Range / area:** 60 ft.; 20-ft. cube · **Components:** V, M
> **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute · **Save:** DEX DC 14 · **Cost:** 1 slot
>
> - **Objects:** objects in the cube are outlined in light. **Creatures:** creatures in the cube are outlined if they fail the Dexterity save.
> - **Affected targets:** shed dim light in a 10-ft. radius and cannot benefit from being invisible.
> - **Attacks:** attack rolls against an affected target have advantage if the attacker can see it. Applies to Nathan, Gordon, and other attackers; advantage does not stack.
> - **Placement:** allies can also fail the save. Choose the cube carefully. No repeat save is listed; moving out of the original cube does not remove the effect.
> - **After casting:** Nathan can attack and command Gordon normally while maintaining concentration.

> [!info]- Grease
> **Cast:** Action · **Range / area:** 60 ft.; 10-ft. square on the ground · **Components:** V, S, M
> **Duration:** 1 minute · **Concentration:** No · **Save:** DEX DC 14 · **Cost:** 1 slot
>
> - **Terrain:** the square becomes difficult terrain; movement through it costs twice as much, even after a successful save.
> - **Saves:** a creature standing in the square when it appears must save or fall prone. A creature entering the area or ending its turn there must also save or fall prone.
> - **Limits:** no damage; does not restrain creatures. They can stand and move out if they have enough movement. Standing costs half their speed.
> - **Allies:** also affected. Keep friendly positions and routes clear when possible.
> - **Attacks against prone creatures:** advantage from within 5 ft.; disadvantage from farther away. Shoot a standing target when practical.
> - **Use:** slow one group at a doorway while the party fights another. Can remain while Nathan concentrates on Faerie Fire.

> [!info]- Magic Missile
> **Cast:** Action · **Range:** 120 ft. · **Components:** V, S, M
> **Duration:** Instantaneous · **Concentration:** No · **Cost:** 1 slot · **Preparation:** always prepared
>
> - **Targets:** choose visible creatures in range. Create three darts, assigning them to one creature or splitting them among several.
> - **Damage:** each dart automatically hits for 1d4 + 1 force damage. All darts strike simultaneously; no attack roll or saving throw.
> - **Defense:** the Shield spell blocks Magic Missile; an ordinary physical shield does not automatically stop it.
> - **Higher slots:** one additional dart per slot level above first.

> [!info]- Thunderwave
> **Cast:** Action · **Range / area:** Self; 15-ft. cube extending from Nathan · **Components:** V, S, M
> **Duration:** Instantaneous · **Concentration:** No · **Save:** CON DC 14 · **Cost:** 1 slot · **Preparation:** always prepared
>
> - **Creatures in the cube:** failed save = 2d8 thunder damage and pushed 10 ft. away from Nathan. Successful save = half damage, rounded down, and no push.
> - **Objects:** unsecured objects fully inside the area are automatically pushed 10 ft. away from Nathan.
> - **Noise:** the thunderous boom is audible up to 300 ft. away.
> - **Placement:** allies can be hit and pushed too. Useful for nearby groups or creating space to retreat.
> - **Higher slots:** add 1d8 damage per slot level above first.

## Other references

> [!info]- Concentration
> - **One at a time:** Nathan can maintain one concentration spell. Starting another ends the first; maintaining it costs no action on later turns.
> - **Normal turns:** Nathan can move, attack, command Gordon, and cast ordinary non-concentration spells while maintaining concentration.
> - **Taking damage:** damage triggers a Constitution saving throw; it does not automatically end concentration.
>     - Roll d20 + 5: Constitution modifier (+3) + saving throw proficiency (+2).
>     - DC = 10 or half the damage taken, rounded down, whichever is higher. Meeting or beating the DC keeps concentration; failure ends it.
>     - Make a separate save for each source of damage.
> - **Other endings:** concentration ends when Nathan becomes incapacitated (including unconscious), dies, or chooses to stop it (no action). The spell also ends when its duration expires.
> - **Long casting times:** Mending takes 1 minute to cast, rather than one action. Nathan must concentrate for that whole minute, even though Mending's finished effect does not require concentration.
>     - Starting Mending ends an active Faerie Fire. During combat, casting Mending takes Nathan's action each turn for 10 rounds.
>     - If concentration breaks before casting finishes, the spell fails. To try again, start the full casting time over; no spell slot is spent on the failed casting.
>     - Casting Detect Magic as a ritual also requires concentration during its extended casting time.

> [!info]- Shared spellcasting reminders
> - **Prepared spells and slots:** preparation is the menu; slots pay for casts. Repeat any prepared spell while slots remain. After a long rest, regain slots and change the five chosen preparations if desired.
> - **Attack vs. save:** use whichever the spell lists.
>     - **Spell attack:** Nathan rolls to hit: d20 + 6 against the target's AC. The +6 comes from Intelligence (+4) + proficiency (+2). Meeting or beating AC hits.
>     - **Spell save:** the target rolls the listed saving throw against Nathan's spell save DC 14: 8 + Intelligence (+4) + proficiency (+2). Nathan does not roll to hit. The spell says what happens on a successful or failed save.
> - **Reactions:** regain your reaction at the start of your turn.
> - **Touch delivery:** Channel Magic changes who delivers the touch, not the spell's action, slot, component, or concentration requirements. Gordon must be in position when Nathan casts.
