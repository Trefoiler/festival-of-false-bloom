## My turn

| Resource | Main options |
| --- | --- |
| **Action** | Attack once, Dash, Disengage, or another standard action |
| **Bonus action** | Enter/end Rage, Frenzy attack on eligible turns, or drink under the table's homebrew |
| **Reaction** | Opportunity attack; refreshes at the start of EscaMundo's turn |
| **Movement** | 30 ft.; split before/after actions as needed |

**Taking damage while raging:** halve bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage, including magical sources; round down. Other damage types are unaffected by ordinary Rage.

### Typical turns

- **Opening turn:** Bonus action to Rage → move into reach → attack.
    - Choose whether to Frenzy when entering Rage. No Frenzy bonus attack on this opening turn.
- **Normal raging turn:** Move as needed → attack. The bonus action remains available for drinking or another option.
- **Frenzied turn:** Attack with the action → attack again with the bonus action.
    - Available starting on the turn after entering Frenzy. Both Strength-based melee weapon hits receive Rage's +2 damage.
- **Escape turn:** Disengage → move away. Check whether Rage ends at the end of the turn.

**Reckless Attack choice:** decide with the first attack on your turn; no extra action needed. Strength-based melee weapon attacks have advantage that turn, but incoming attack rolls have advantage until your next turn.

- **Shield versus both hands:** a shield adds 2 AC; using both hands increases the axe's average damage by 1 per hit. The attack bonus stays the same.
- **Bonuses:** +5 to hit = Strength (+3) + proficiency (+2). Rage adds 2 damage, not 2 to hit. Homebrew drunkenness bonuses are separate.
- **Critical hit:** double the attack's damage dice; add the fixed modifier once. Rage's +2 is not doubled.
- **Attack advantage:** Rage itself does not grant it. Reckless Attack is a separate choice; multiple sources of advantage do not stack.

## Other references

> [!info]- Rage
> - **Activation:** bonus action; 3 uses per long rest. Lasts up to 1 minute (10 rounds).
> - **Damage:** +2 on melee weapon attacks using Strength. This is already included in the raging damage column above.
> - **Resistance:** half bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage, rounded down; magical sources still qualify by damage type.
> - **Advantage:** Strength checks and Strength saving throws, not attack rolls.
> - **Armor:** these benefits require no heavy armor. Light/medium armor and shields are compatible.
> - **Ends early:** unconsciousness, or the end of your turn if you have neither attacked a hostile creature nor taken damage since your previous turn.
>     - A missed attack still counts as attacking. Taking damage alone can also keep Rage going, within its maximum duration.
>     - You can voluntarily end Rage with a bonus action on your turn.
> - **Spellcasting:** cannot cast or concentrate on spells while raging.

> [!info]- Reckless Attack
> - **Decision:** when making your first attack on your turn. No bonus action, Rage use, or other resource cost.
> - **Benefit:** advantage on melee weapon attack rolls using Strength for the rest of that turn, including an eligible Frenzy attack.
> - **Tradeoff:** attack rolls against you have advantage until your next turn, including ranged attacks.
> - **Separate feature:** can be used without Rage; Rage can be used without Reckless Attack.

> [!info]- Frenzy
> - **Choice:** opt in when entering Rage; ordinary Rage does not require Frenzy.
> - **Benefit:** one melee weapon attack as a bonus action on each of your turns after the activation turn, while that Rage lasts.
> - **Cost:** gain 1 level of exhaustion when that Rage ends. This also applies if the Rage ends early.
> - **Action economy:** only one bonus action per turn. A Frenzy attack competes with drinking or another bonus action.
> - **Attack count:** at level 3, the ordinary Attack action gives one attack. A Frenzy bonus attack is a separate second attack, not Extra Attack.

> [!info]- Danger Sense
> - **Benefit:** advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects you can see, such as visible traps or spells.
> - **Unavailable:** while blinded, deafened, or incapacitated.
> - **Base roll:** d20 + 2; when advantage applies, roll two d20s and use the higher result.
