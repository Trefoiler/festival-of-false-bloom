# Tomas Reed

**Updated through:** [Session 2](../Session%20Notes/Session%202.md)
**Index:** [People & Groups](index.md)

- **Identity:** Owner of the farm visited for the quest **A Hospitable Choice**; the previously unidentified wounded man against the table.
    - First name explicitly spelled without an H; surname confirmed as Reed.
    - His wife Betty had died recently. The supply-room corpse was not explicitly identified in the recording.
- **Relationship with the party:** Traveling ally; restored EscaMundo after the farmhouse battle and agreed to accompany the supply delivery.
    - Nathan repaired his door and window; Gordon cleared the attackers' bodies from the house.
    - Tomas allowed the party to stay overnight and supplied the cart and his ox, Stevenwald.
- **Heirloom stone:** Hidden in his sword; described as dark and dull.
    - Said his family had guarded it for generations. His father insisted on protecting it without explaining its purpose and warned that knowing more could be dangerous.
    - After the ape sought the “stone of change,” Tomas showed it to the party. Nathan examined it with Jorah's help and concluded someone was captive inside, then told Tomas.
    - Its powers, occupant, and formal identity remain unknown. The ape's target and this stone have not been conclusively equated.
- **During the river encounter:** Became rigid and unresponsive as shadows emerged around him and Stevenwald.
    - Recovered afterward with only a fuzzy impression of something long ago; did not understand what the party had fought.
- **Other information:** Described a supply cave north of Hanesworth, toward the mountains, arranged by Silas.
    - Warned against traveling through the woods at night. His comments about pollution, outsiders, and local news are his statements, not independently established facts.
    - Did not identify the farmhouse attackers' affiliation.
- **Character moments:** Mocked Nathan's name, awkwardly attempted a high five, and claimed sobriety before the party found two liquor bottles beneath a floorboard; “Sober Tommy” became a joke.
- **Last seen:** With Stevenwald and the supply cart beside the river, returning toward Hanesworth after revealing the stone.
- **Sources:** [Session 1](../Session%20Notes/Session%201.md); [Session 2](../Session%20Notes/Session%202.md).
