# Riverside Fey

**Updated through:** [Session 2](../Session%20Notes/Session%202.md)
**Index:** [People & Groups](index.md)

- **Identity:** Unnamed fey, described at the table as fairies; encountered across the river on the return from Tomas's farm.
- **Observed ritual:** Gathered around a dead deer. Nathan recognized summoning magic; a dark opening formed and an ape emerged from the carcass.
    - Jorah addressed them in Sylvan. They turned toward him and shushed him.
    - Disappeared into the brush as the creature emerged; not confirmed killed or captured.
- **Associated encounter:** The ape searched Tomas's cart for a stone, later called the “stone of change.” Two shadows formed around Tomas and Stevenwald.
    - The ape and shadows were defeated, but the fey's motives and the mechanics of the summoning remain unknown.
- **Unconfirmed links:** Jorah's missing animals, Garrick Voss, and the farmhouse attackers. None is established by this encounter alone.
- **Last seen:** Vanishing into the riverside brush during the ritual.
- **Source:** [Session 2](../Session%20Notes/Session%202.md).
