- **Tomas's account:** His family has guarded the stone for generations.
    - His father demanded its protection and warned that learning more could be dangerous. Tomas said he had not been told its purpose.
    - Nathan told Tomas his conclusion that someone was inside; identity, imprisonment, and safe handling remain unresolved.
- **Ritual and shadows:** Fey performed a ritual around a dead deer; an ape emerged from the carcass and the fey disappeared into the brush.
    - Two shadows emerged around Tomas and Stevenwald while they were unresponsive.
    - The ape rose in a shadow form after its first defeat. All three enemies were ultimately defeated; the mechanism linking them remains unexplained.
- **Next:** Learn who was being summoned, who wanted the stone, and whether the fey, stone, and animal disturbances share a cause.
