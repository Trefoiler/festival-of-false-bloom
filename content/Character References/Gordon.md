## His turn

**Timing:** Gordon takes his turn immediately after Nathan, on the same initiative count.

| Resource     | What Gordon can do                                                                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Movement** | Move on his own; no command needed. Walking and flying share his available movement.  |
| **Action**   | Dodge unless Nathan spends a bonus action on Nathan's turn to command another action. |
| **Reaction** | Use on his own; refreshes at the start of Gordon's turn. Channel Magic uses this.     |
- **Default turn:** Nathan commands Force Strike → Gordon moves into range and attacks.
- **Support alternative:** Nathan commands Help → Gordon moves within 5 ft. of the enemy and distracts it.
- **Without a command:** Gordon can move and takes Dodge. If Nathan is incapacitated, Gordon can choose any action instead.

## Actions

> [!tip] Force Strike
> **Ranged weapon attack:** +6 to hit  
> **Range:** 30 ft. · **Target:** one target Gordon can see  
> **Hit:** 1d4 + 2 force damage
>
> **Cost:** Nathan's bonus action to command; Gordon's action to attack. Uses Nathan's spell attack bonus, even though Force Strike is a weapon attack.
> **Close range:** disadvantage if a hostile creature within 5 ft. can see Gordon and isn't incapacitated.

> [!tip] Help: distract an enemy
> **Cost:** Nathan's bonus action to command; Gordon's action on his turn.
>
> - **Position:** within 5 ft. of the enemy, not just beside the ally. Choose the enemy to distract; no ally needs to be designated.
> - **Benefit:** the next allied attack roll against that enemy before Gordon's next turn has advantage.
>     - Used on that roll, whether it hits or misses. Does not benefit every attack or stack with existing advantage.
>     - Nathan can benefit on his next turn if no other ally uses it first.
> - **Risk:** Gordon is exposed beside the enemy. Flying out of its reach can provoke an opportunity attack; he has no Flyby trait.
>     - Disengage uses Gordon's action instead of Help that turn.

## Reactions

> [!tip] Channel Magic
> **Position:** Gordon must already be able to touch the recipient and be within 120 ft. of Nathan when Nathan casts a Touch spell.
> **Cost:** Nathan casts using his own action, spell slot if needed, and normal spell requirements; Gordon spends his reaction to deliver the touch. No bonus-action command required.
>
> | Nathan's spell | Nathan's cost | Delivered effect |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | Cure Wounds | Action + 1 spell slot | Heal the ally Gordon touches. |
> | Shocking Grasp | Action; no spell slot | Nathan's melee spell attack through Gordon; a hit deals lightning damage and prevents the target's reactions until the target's next turn starts. |
>
> **Action choice:** either spell uses Nathan's action instead of his Lightning Launcher attack. His bonus action remains available to command Gordon's next action.

> [!example]- Example: Cure Wounds through Gordon, then attack
> 1. Gordon is already beside the ally and within 120 ft. of Nathan.
> 2. On Nathan's turn, Nathan casts Cure Wounds, spending his action and one spell slot.
> 3. Gordon spends his reaction to deliver the healing.
> 4. Nathan spends his bonus action to command Gordon to attack.
> 5. Gordon's turn begins immediately afterward. Refresh his reaction, then move and take the commanded action.
>
> If Gordon needs to reach the ally first, position him on an earlier turn. Moving out of an enemy's reach may provoke an opportunity attack.

## Other references

> [!info]- Repair & recreation
> - **Short rest:** after at least 1 hour, spend available Hit Dice one at a time. Each restores 1d4 + 1 HP, up to maximum HP; reduce Hit Dice remaining for each die spent.
> - **Long rest (table rule):** restore full HP and all spent Hit Dice, using 2024 recovery. Set Hit Dice remaining to its maximum.
> - **Mending:** restore 2d6 HP per 1-minute cast, up to maximum HP. No spell slot or Hit Dice spent; repeat as needed. Nathan must concentrate while casting.
> - **Death:** Gordon can die. His body vanishes if he or Nathan dies, leaving the heart where Gordon was.
> - **Recreate after a long rest:** with Nathan alive, touch the recovered Amber Heart and apply Homunculus Servant again. No spell slot; Gordon still occupies one active infusion.
> - **Lost heart:** a replacement gem or crystal must be worth at least 100 gp. Without a suitable heart, Gordon cannot be recreated.
> - **During combat:** recreation is not a combat revive. Mending repairs a living Gordon; it does not recreate his vanished body. Cure Wounds does not heal constructs such as Gordon.
