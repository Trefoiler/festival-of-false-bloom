## Session 2

- **Ox thieves:** Two goblins found leading Stevenwald away from the farm.
    - Fled when Tomas charged with his sword; no second farm battle occurred.
- **Betty:** Tomas's recently deceased wife, mentioned when Jorah chose the couple's bed for the night.
    - Tomas said she had died about a day earlier. The corpse discovered in Session 1 was not explicitly identified as her.
- **Ape and shadows:** Defeated beside the river while escorting Tomas's cart.
    - The ape emerged from a deer carcass during the fey ritual, searched for the “stone of change,” and counterattacked Azier in melee.
    - Two shadows emerged around Tomas and Stevenwald. One visibly drained Jorah and healed itself; Gordon destroyed one shadow and EscaMundo killed the other.
    - Azier dropped the ape's first form. It rose again as a shadow ape, downed him, and was killed by EscaMundo.
    - Names, allegiance, and the mechanism linking the creatures were not established. See [Riverside Fey](Riverside%20Fey.md).
