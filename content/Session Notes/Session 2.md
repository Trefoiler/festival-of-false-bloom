**Date:** 2026-10-19

## Quick recap

- The party defeated the farmhouse attackers. The wounded farmer, Tomas Reed, restored EscaMundo after his death-save sequence.
- Recovered Tomas's ox, Stevenwald, and rested at the farm. Tomas then loaded the supply cart and joined the escort toward Hanesworth.
- A riverside fey ritual produced an ape from a dead deer; two shadows emerged around Tomas and Stevenwald. The ape sought the “stone of change.”
- The party defeated the shadows and the ape, including its second shadow form. Azier was downed during the fight and recovered afterward.
- Tomas revealed a family stone concealed in his sword. Nathan's examination led him to conclude someone was trapped inside. Everyone leveled to 3 after the adventure stopped.

## Chronological summary

- **Farmhouse battle:**
    - Damane was absent for this fight. Azier's absence was explained afterward as the cult fanatic having knocked him out, replacing the earlier joke that he ran away.
    - The five opponents were a cult fanatic, two goblins, and two bandits. Jorah's Entangle trapped both goblins and delayed their attacks.
    - EscaMundo smashed a bottle over a bandit, fought the fanatic, and struck a fleeing bandit with an opportunity attack. The fanatic's spells and dagger attacks brought him down.
    - Nathan fought from outside the window with Fire Bolt, later switching to his dagger against a goblin. Gordon supported him with Force Strike.
    - Jorah became a panther, killed the fleeing bandit, and fought the fanatic. Hold Person briefly interrupted him; damage forced him out of Wild Shape.
    - Nathan's missed Fire Bolt ignited dry grass outside. The fight spread into the yard; all five attackers were eventually killed, with Nathan finishing the fanatic.
- **Meeting Tomas Reed:**
    - The wounded man was the farm's owner. Despite his own injury, Tomas attended EscaMundo and restored him at 1 HP after the table had declared him dead during the death-save sequence.
    - Damane joined afterward and Azier rejoined the group.
    - Tomas described a supply cave north of Hanesworth, toward the mountains, arranged by Silas. Nathan showed him the quest **A Hospitable Choice** to explain the request for medical supplies.
    - Two more goblins were leading away Tomas's ox. Tomas charged out with his sword, the goblins fled, and the party recovered Stevenwald.
- **Overnight at the farm:**
    - Tomas warned that the woods were dangerous after dark and agreed to travel back to town with the party.
    - Gordon dragged the attackers' bodies outside. With EscaMundo holding the door, Nathan repaired its hinges using Mending; he also repaired the broken window.
    - Nathan and EscaMundo uncovered two bottles of hidden liquor beneath a floorboard. EscaMundo later made a bottle from his grapes, collected more ingredients, and took parts from the press for a future portable setup.
    - The party completed a long rest. Tomas prepared Stevenwald and loaded the supplies into the cart for the return journey.
- **Ritual by the river:**
    - During the morning journey, Nathan recognized magical activity across the river. Fey were performing a summoning around a dead deer.
    - Jorah called to them in Sylvan. They shushed him; a dark opening and large arms appeared, and the fey disappeared into the brush as an ape emerged from the carcass.
    - Tomas and Stevenwald became rigid and unresponsive as shadows stretched out and formed two creatures near them.
    - The ape rushed the cart and searched the supplies, knocking Gordon aside. Jorah used Speak with Animals: it demanded a stone, later described as the “stone of change.”
    - Jorah offered an ordinary stone; the ape rejected it and accused him of lying when told the sought stone was absent. Azier attacked with his staff; the ape intercepted him and threw him back.
- **Ape and shadows battle:**
    - Jorah summoned Bear Spirit, granting the group and Gordon 7 temporary HP, then became a jaguar. The shadows' attacks forced him back into elf form.
    - Azier held the ape's attention with staff attacks and unarmed strikes. The ape counterattacked, threw combatants, and hurled a hard lump of dung that missed him.
    - EscaMundo fought one shadow while Nathan and Gordon fought the other. Gordon destroyed his target with Force Strike; Nathan then positioned him to help the injured allies.
    - One shadow visibly drained Jorah and recovered from its own injuries.
    - Azier brought the ape down, but it convulsed, its jaw reformed, and it rose in a shadow form. It then knocked Azier unconscious; his first death save was a natural 1, giving two failures.
    - EscaMundo finished the remaining shadow and then killed the shadow ape with his axe. Azier recovered after combat; the proposed Cure Wounds delivery through Gordon was not carried out.
- **Tomas's heirloom:**
    - Tomas and Stevenwald recovered with little understanding of the battle. Tomas mentioned a fuzzy memory of something long ago.
    - On hearing about the stone, Tomas explained that his family had guarded an heirloom for generations. His father had insisted on protecting it and warned that knowing more could be dangerous.
    - Tomas removed a dull, dark stone from his sword. With Jorah helping, Nathan examined it using Arcana and concluded there was someone captive inside.
    - Nathan asked whether Tomas wanted to know, then told him. Its occupant, purpose, and connection to the summoning remain unresolved.
    - The adventure ended before reaching town. Jon awarded level 3 during the discussion afterward.

## Clues & open questions

- **The stone of change:** The ape's stated target, as relayed through Jorah and repeated afterward.
    - Tomas's sword contains an heirloom stone; it is the leading candidate, but no identification or full explanation established its powers or formal name.
    - Who is inside, and why was Tomas's family told to guard it without learning more?
- **Riverside fey:** Performed the ritual and disappeared when the ape emerged.
    - Why use an animal carcass, and who directed the search for the stone?
- **Tomas and Stevenwald's shadows:** The creatures emerged around them while they were unresponsive.
    - What caused this, and how did the ape's second form relate to the shadows?
- **Farmhouse insignia:** The attackers' knives matched Silas's chest mark, but the fight provided no explanation of the connection.
    - Tomas did not identify the intruders' affiliation. Garrick's responsibility remains unverified.
- **Supply cave:** Tomas described Silas's storage arrangement north of Hanesworth.
    - It may relate to the quest **A Cavern's Contingency**; the party has not verified that it is the same cave.
- **Jorah's investigation:** The ritual and deer carcass add another unnatural animal event to investigate.
    - A connection to his earlier missing animals and disturbed spirits is plausible, not established.
