# Session 1: Festival attack & the farm

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Active players:** Owen, Damane, Shepherd, Brayden
**References:** [Party](../Party.md) · [Current Threads](../Current%20Threads.md) · [People](../People.md)
**Source:** Session notes and campaign handouts.

## Quick recap

- Hanesworth's flower festival exploded; the party joined forces and defeated five attackers wearing black boots.
- Mayor Silas Pruitt named Garrick Voss as the suspected culprit and revealed an extortion demand and a restrictive chest sigil.
- The party chose the quest **A Hospitable Choice**, traveling to a farm for supplies and medical aid.
- Five armed men occupied the farmhouse; their knives bore the same insignia as Silas's sigil.

## Where we stopped

- **Farmhouse, at night:** Confrontation about to begin.
	- Azier entered the main room from the adjoining supply room.
	- EscaMundo forced the barricaded front door open and moved to its entrance.
	- Nathan remained outside the window Gordon had shattered.
	- Jorah was outside near a window.
	- Five enemies were on the ground with knives drawn; no surprise round.
	- D&D Beyond Maps retains the party's last positions for reference.
- **Immediate stakes:**
	- A man inside was bleeding from a side wound and needed help.
	- Food and medical supplies were located in the adjoining room.
	- EscaMundo had returned injured after being kidnapped; his character sheet tracks his HP.

## Chronological summary

- **Arrival at the festival:**
	- Nathan and Gordon had arrived the previous night; Nathan wanted materials and found the festival crowds inconvenient.
	- EscaMundo had been drinking in the tavern.
	- Azier visited following his master Toshiro's recommendation.
	- Jorah followed wagon tracks associated with wastefully slaughtered animals into town, losing the trail among the crowd.
- **Festival attack:**
	- Mayor Silas Pruitt began his welcome speech.
	- Flowers and a tavern barrel glowed and hummed before exploding.
	- EscaMundo escaped through a window; the barkeep and many others died.
	- Men wearing black leather boots attacked fleeing civilians and guards.
- **First fight:**
	- The party defeated three bandits and two priest acolytes.
	- Jorah:
		- Bear Spirit Totem granted 7 temporary HP to the party, including Gordon.
		- Ice Knife killed one acolyte.
	- Nathan and Gordon:
		- Nathan used Faerie Fire, then Fire Bolt.
		- Gordon attacked with Force Strike and killed the final bandit.
	- Azier fought with his quarterstaff and unarmed strikes, killing a bandit.
	- EscaMundo raged and killed a bandit and the remaining acolyte with his axe.
- **Aftermath and introductions:**
	- The survivors were grieving amid extensive destruction; the town guards had been killed.
	- Nathan found 2 gp; EscaMundo recovered a bottle of mead and drank some.
	- The party exchanged names.
		- Nathan explained Earth and that Gordon was a construct resembling a raccoon from home.
		- Jorah described disappearing animals, abnormal behavior, and disturbed spirits near his home.
	- Nathan noticed no animals visible in town.
- **Questioning Silas:**
	- Silas emerged alive from the rubble, bruised and shaken.
	- Nathan noticed he was withholding information.
	- Jorah used Entangle; EscaMundo threatened him and pulled him free roughly.
	- Silas described Garrick Voss's extortion demand and showed his chest sigil.
		- He offered guidance toward Garrick and said the party needed about 20 supplies each for the journey.
		- See [Current Threads](../Current%20Threads.md#Garrick%20Voss%20%26%20the%20Hollowed%20Expanse) for his claims.
- **Quest selection and travel:**
	- Silas supplied five quest scrolls; Nathan kept them.
	- The party considered the bridge caravan because of Jorah's search and the black boots.
	- Chose the quest **A Hospitable Choice** instead.
		- Jorah hoped to find animals; EscaMundo wanted alcohol from the farm's vineyard.
	- Departed east and arrived around nightfall.
- **Farm investigation:**
	- Overturned wagons and disorder suggested the farm had been disturbed.
	- EscaMundo collected remaining grapes while the others approached the house.
	- Nathan saw a man sitting against a table, breathing heavily.
	- Azier failed twice to kick down the front door.
		- Slipped on the first attempt and took 1 damage.
	- Jorah spotted a shape in the rafters, became a rat, and entered through a cracked window.
		- Found five cloaked men with daggers above the main room.
		- Their black footwear differed from the town attackers' boots.
		- The injured man had a bleeding side wound; the front door was barricaded.
		- An adjoining room held food, medical supplies, a gold pouch, and a corpse.
	- Jorah reverted, pocketed the gold pouch, opened a window, and reported his findings.
	- Nathan proposed having Gordon carry supplies out through the window.
		- This plan was not carried out.
- **Kidnapping and breach:**
	- EscaMundo disappeared while separated from the party.
		- Nathan heard movement in the woods; Gordon scouted but could not see through the thick trees.
		- EscaMundo later returned running from the trees, visibly scraped up.
	- Jorah knocked over a box while leaving through the window.
		- The men descended from the rafters, alerted to the party.
	- Azier entered from the supply room; Gordon broke the window; EscaMundo forced the front door open.
	- The men drew knives bearing the same insignia as Silas's chest sigil.
		- Session ended before initiative or attacks in this encounter.

## Clues & open questions

- **Matching insignia:** Observed on Silas's chest and the farmhouse knives.
	- The connection is established; its meaning and the men's affiliation remain unknown.
- **Garrick's involvement:** Silas's explanation, not independently verified.
	- What was the additional demand, and how does the sigil enforce secrecy?
- **Animal disturbances:** Jorah's account plus Nathan's observation in town.
	- Animals encountered in the woods appeared fine; no link to the explosion is confirmed.
- **Caravans:**
	- Is Jorah's tracked caravan the one at the western bridge?
	- Its connection to the town attack is unconfirmed.
- **Farm occupants:**
	- Who are the injured man and the dead person, and what happened here?
	- Who kidnapped EscaMundo, and what happened in the woods?

- **Mayor Silas Pruitt:** Offered help against Garrick after a hostile interrogation.
